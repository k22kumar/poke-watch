# Music

The game's music is the adaptive score in [adaptive/](adaptive/README.md): files, thresholds, limitations and reproduction are documented there.

The licensed-recording slots (`manifest.json`) and the generated chip-tune score they replaced were removed in the hero-shooter pivot (`docs/hero-shooter-pivot.md` Q51). Every music state plays the adaptive score. The old mechanism is recoverable from the `survivor-final` tag.
