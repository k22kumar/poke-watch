# Species identity

What each Pokémon in Poke-Watch is in HGSS, and how the survival game adapts it. Canonical facts and game balance are kept apart: the importer writes HGSS data to `canon` on each species (abilities without hidden ones, the HGSS level-up list filtered to moves the game implements, evolution level, height, weight), while game values live in `learnset`, `coverage`, `evolveLevel` and the rules. Everything here is regenerated by `npm run content` from `content/roster.json` and PokéAPI; the tables below were generated from the built data.

## Evolutions

Each partner evolves twice, to its final form (`"partnerEvolutions": 2` in the roster), at the HGSS levels. In bot runs the first evolution happens in region 1 and the final one in region 3 (Feraligatr at 11:56, Lv 30; Meganium at 14:39, Lv 32).

## Abilities

One ability per species: the first of its HGSS abilities that has a survival adaptation (`abilityOf`, `src/rules/abilities.ts`). Partners show theirs on the partner select screen and in the HUD when it triggers; wild Pokémon carry theirs as an enemy trait, with a short popup when it matters (for example "Levitate" when a Ground move does nothing to a Gastly).

| Ability | HGSS | In Poke-Watch |
|---|---|---|
| Overgrow / Blaze / Torrent | Type moves ×1.5 at ≤ 1/3 HP | Same, with an aura in the type's colour and a HUD chip |
| Levitate | Immune to Ground moves | Immune to Ground damage |
| Flash Fire | Immune to Fire; boosts own Fire moves | Immune to Fire damage; its attacks then deal ×1.5 |
| Water Absorb | Water heals 1/4 max HP | Same |
| Dry Skin | Water heals 1/4; Fire ×1.25 | Same |
| Sturdy | Gen IV: blocks OHKO moves | Survives one lethal hit at 1 HP from full HP (the Gen V reading; there are no OHKO moves) |
| Magma Armor | Can't be frozen | Immune to chill (the game's freeze) |
| Early Bird | Wakes twice as fast | Sleep lasts half as long |
| Vital Spirit | Can't sleep | Immune to sleep |
| Guts | Attack ×1.5 while statused | Contact damage ×1.5 while statused |
| Swarm | Bug moves ×1.5 at ≤ 1/3 HP | Contact damage ×1.5 at ≤ 1/3 HP |
| Static | 30 % paralysis on contact | 30 % to paralyse you (half speed, 1.5 s) when your contact move hits it |
| Effect Spore | 30 % poison, paralysis or sleep on contact | 30 % paralysis only (the player has no poison or sleep status) |
| Chlorophyll | Speed ×2 in sun | Walks twice as fast while Fire weather is active |
| Pressure | Opponent's moves cost 2 PP | Within 12 m, your cooldowns recover 15 % slower (there is no PP) |

Not adapted, with the reason: Run Away (no fleeing), Rock Head (no recoil moves on enemies), Sand Veil and Snow Cloak (no sandstorm or hail), Damp (nothing self-destructs), Flame Body (the player has no burn status), Hustle (attacks never miss), Oblivious (no infatuation or taunt), Inner Focus (no flinching). Sandshrew, Swinub and Zubat therefore have no ability in game.

## Learnsets

The six partner lines have curated learnsets (`learnsets` in `content/roster.json`). Every move must appear in the line's HGSS level-up list; the importer checks this and rejects anything else. Game levels are compressed to fit a 20-minute run, so they're lower than the HGSS levels, but the order follows HGSS where it can. Moves the game doesn't implement (status moves such as Leech Seed, Growl or Smokescreen) are left out of the HGSS lists below.

Coverage offers are moves of another type the line learns in HGSS. They're only offered once the player's level reaches the HGSS level, so Charmander can't be offered Shadow Claw early.

## Move mechanics the game doesn't model

Moves are classified into attack shapes from their data (power, class, priority, accuracy, ailment, drain, multi-hit, targeting). These HGSS mechanics have no equivalent and are ignored, so the move keeps its damage and shape only:

- Two-turn charging (Solar Beam, Skull Bash): fires on its normal cooldown after the shape's short wind-up (0.12 s for beams); there's no charging turn.
- Recoil (Take Down, Double-Edge, Flare Blitz): no recoil damage.
- Lock-in moves (Thrash, Petal Dance, Outrage): no lock-in or confusion afterward.
- Stat drops on the user (Superpower): none.
- Fixed damage (Dragon Rage, 40 HP in HGSS): has no power in the data, so it's treated as power 60 and scales with stats like any other move.
- Rollout's growing power: a dash with normal damage.
- PP: replaced by cooldowns.
